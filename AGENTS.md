# Exterior model rules

- Architectural plans, elevations, and details control dimensions, openings,
  siding, trim, and placement. Preserve their source bindings and uncertainty.
- **Do not use the rendered house image stored elsewhere as
  `references/source/exterior-reference.jpeg` as source material.** Mason
  explicitly rejected this image on 2026-10-17. The same image attached in chat
  is also excluded. Do not use it for geometry, finishes, colors, or design.
- User screenshots identify defects; they do not establish construction details.
  Record unsupported colors, profiles, and hidden details as provisional.
- Keep private plans, authoring files, photographs, and QA evidence in ignored
  local folders. Do not publish or push this architectural work.
- Generate panels, battens, corner boards, casing, and bands from shared wall
  boundaries and openings. Respect siding changes, real openings, and joints.
  Never cover an incorrect assembly with oversized trim or extra cover panels.
- Inspect every affected facade after changing shared generators. Regeneration
  must retain repairs without duplicate objects or a separate manual cleanup.
- Inspect Blender output and the exact browser export in neutral materials,
  original materials, final lighting, close views, and a moving full orbit.
  Successful scripts, exports, and numeric checks alone do not prove correctness.
- Record unresolved defects and evidence gaps. Report only checks actually run.
- Follow [the validation procedure](docs/model-validation.md) when repairing or
  regenerating architecture. Preserve unrelated work and prior source assets.
